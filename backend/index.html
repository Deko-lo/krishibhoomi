RURAL LAND CHALLENGES

Why Agricultural Land Resurvey Matters

Outdated British-era chain surveys and undocumented generational land divisions trigger boundary disputes and credit delays for millions of farmers.

📍

Unclear Boundaries

Natural hedges and bunds shift over generations, resulting in ambiguous borders and crop destruction disputes.

🗺️

Outdated Records

Paper khasra maps do not reflect recent inheritances, partitions, canal diversions, or road acquisitions.

⚠️

Area Discrepancies

Recorded revenue title area often differs from actual GPS ground measurements, hindering bank loans and subsidies.

⚔️

Boundary Disputes

Lack of geo-referenced boundary benchmarks leads to prolonged civil litigation between neighboring landholders.

🏛️

Slow Verification

Manual measurement processes take months without priority scoring or transparent tracking for small farmers.
